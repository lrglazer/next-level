Privacy Policy

Last updated November 11, 2026

Short version: we collect what you give us to run the app, we don't sell your personal information, and you can delete your account and its data any time.

What we collect

  • Account info: name, email, password (stored only as a secure hash), account type, school, sport, position and class year.
  • Profile and posts: photos, stats, highlights, props, comments and your NIL rate card.
  • Messages and deals: DMs and NIL offer details, so both sides can see them.
  • Basic technical data: IP address and request logs, used for security and to stop abuse.

How we use it

  • To run Next Level: show your profile and posts, deliver messages and offers, and keep you signed in.
  • To keep the platform safe and compliant: block banned deal categories, apply rate limits, and handle reports.
  • To contact you about your account or important changes.

We do not sell your personal information and we don't run third-party ad trackers.

Who can see what

  • Your profile, posts and stats are visible to other Next Level users.
  • DMs are visible only to the people in the conversation.
  • An NIL offer is visible to the brand that sent it and the athlete who received it.
  • We may share information if the law requires it, or to protect users from harm.

Where it's stored

Data is stored on servers run by our hosting provider, Render, in the United States. Fonts load from Google Fonts.

Your choices

  • Edit your profile any time.
  • Delete your account from your profile settings; your account and what you posted are removed from the app.
  • Email us to ask what data we hold about you.

Age

Next Level is for people 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone younger. If you think a minor has an account, tell us and we'll remove it.

Changes

We'll update the date above when this policy changes and let you know in the app about important changes.
